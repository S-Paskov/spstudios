Privacy Policy

Application: Chronostone (com.SP.gemquest)
Last updated: 15 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Chronostone (the "Game", "we", "us") handles information when you install and use the Game on Android devices. By using the Game you agree to the practices described below.

1. Summary

The Game does not show advertising, does not use an advertising identifier, and does not include any third-party analytics or crash-reporting service. It does not ask you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or postal address.

Data leaves your device in only one situation: when you make an in-app purchase, or when the Game connects to Unity Gaming Services to keep your purchases valid.

2. Information we collect

  • Anonymous player identifier — when the Game starts, Unity Gaming Services assigns an anonymous ID so that your purchases can be tied to your installation. This identifier is not linked to your name, email address, or any Google account.
  • Purchase records — when you buy an item, Google Play Billing confirms the purchase and Unity Cloud Code validates the receipt so the purchased items can be granted to you.
  • Basic technical data required to deliver the service — such as your IP address, which the service provider necessarily receives in order to respond to network requests.

Gameplay data stays on your device. The Game keeps its own local diagnostic log of gameplay events (level progress, session information) in device storage. This log is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its storage deletes it.

3. How we use the information

  • To process and validate in-app purchases and to grant the items you bought.
  • To restore your purchases if you reinstall the Game.
  • To detect fraudulent or invalid purchase receipts.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

4. Third-party services

The Game includes software from the following providers, which process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity Gaming Services — anonymous authentication, in-app purchasing, and Cloud Code receipt validation. See the Unity Player Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing — processes all purchases. See the Google Privacy Policy.

The Game does not include Google AdMob, Firebase, or any other advertising, analytics, or attribution SDK.

5. In-app purchases

All purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment card details, bank information, or billing address. Refunds are handled according to Google Play's refund policy.

6. Advertising

The Game currently contains no advertising and does not access your device's advertising identifier. Should we introduce advertising in a future version, we will update this policy and the Game's Google Play listing before that version is released.

7. Children

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data retention and security

Purchase records and anonymous player identifiers are retained by Unity Gaming Services and Google Play according to their own retention schedules, and for as long as needed to honour your purchases. Local game data remains on your device until you remove it. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the limited data we handle, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

You can delete your data yourself, from inside the Game. Open Chronostone, go to Settings, and tap Delete my data. This removes your anonymous player profile and the purchase records attached to it, immediately and without contacting us. See Delete your data for the full details.

For any other request, contact us using the details in Section 11.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted at this address with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Game after a change takes effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
